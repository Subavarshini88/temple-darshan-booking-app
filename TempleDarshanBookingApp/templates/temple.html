About the Temple

The Tirumala Venkateswara Temple is a famous Hindu temple situated on the Tirumala hills of Tirupati in Andhra Pradesh. The temple is dedicated to Venkateswara, a form of Vishnu, who is believed to have appeared here to save mankind from the trials of Kali Yuga.

The temple is one of the most visited pilgrimage centres in the world and the most visited religious site in India, with an estimated 50,000 to 100,000 pilgrims visiting daily.

Guidelines for Devotees

✦ Traditional attire is mandatory. Men should wear dhoti or pant-shirt; women should wear saree or salwar.
✦ Mobile phones and cameras are strictly not allowed inside the sanctum.
✦ Please maintain silence and decorum inside the temple premises.
✦ Report 30 minutes before your booked darshan slot.
✦ Carry a valid photo ID proof for verification at the entry gate.
